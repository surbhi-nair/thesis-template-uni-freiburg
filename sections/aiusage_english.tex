\section*{Statement on AI Usage}

\bigskip
\bigskip
\bigskip

Large language models (LLMs) are part of the approach studied in this thesis. This statement, however, concerns only the use of AI tools in writing this thesis.

Commercial LLMs (Claude and ChatGPT) were primarily used to check the text of this thesis for spelling and grammar errors, for the use of commas and hyphens, and for the consistent use of American English, as required by the writing guidelines of the Chair of Algorithms and Data Structures. In addition, ChatGPT was used to identify long or nested sentences and to suggest how these could be divided into simpler ones.

Claude was further used as an interactive assistant to understand the publications discussed in the related work in Section 2 and to arrange them chronologically. This informed the structure of that section. All statements about these works are based on the original publications.

These tools were neither used to write or rephrase complete text passages of this thesis, nor used as a source of factual information. All factual claims are based on the cited literature or on the analysis of the experiments conducted as part of this thesis.
